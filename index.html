The story of Yak

Why a Yak?

The Yak is a nomadic, strong, and reliable companion across Himalayan trails. Just like a Yak, we journey across borders, bringing flavours from Tibet, India, China, the Middle East, and the world, into one warm, delicious stop.

That's Yak Express. Taste that brings us together.
